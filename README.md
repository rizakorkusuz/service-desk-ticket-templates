# service-desk-ticket-templates

Copy-ready templates and reference material for writing clear tickets, handing off work and documenting knowledge: the habits that make a service desk fast and a hand-off painless.

**Roles this supports:** Service Desk Analyst, Help Desk Specialist, IT Support Specialist, Desktop Support Technician, Technical Support Specialist and Engineer.

## How to use

1. Copy a template into your ticketing tool (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Freshservice, Zendesk or similar) as a canned response, record template or form.
2. Replace the example SLA times and categories with those from your own service-level agreement.
3. Keep one issue per ticket, write for the next person, and never store passwords or sensitive personal data in a ticket.

## Notes

- The matrix follows common ITIL-style practice (impact times urgency). The timings are examples for learning, not a standard.
- Sample articles use synthetic scenarios. No company data is included.
