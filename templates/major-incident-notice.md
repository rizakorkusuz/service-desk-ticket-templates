# Major incident or outage notice

Copy the block below into your ticketing tool and fill in the fields. Remove lines that do not apply. Never include passwords or personal data.

```text
Subject: [Incident] <service> - <short status>
Status: Investigating / Identified / Monitoring / Resolved
Start time:
Impact: <who and what is affected>
Workaround:
Actions underway:
Next update: <time>
```
